# stem-racing-car
I am lead engineering designing a car for STEM racing professional class, which is a competition for under 19's where you make a car and appropriate portfolios with a team, which then get judged and raced, in order to move up through different levels of competition. (regionals, nationals and worlds)
The car is still in the design phase so no video of the manufactured car, However i have attached a few of the design files.
The project does not need funding, as the team uses money from sponsors for the manufacture of the car.
In the read me screenshots you can see iterative changes made to the car throughout the design process so far.
